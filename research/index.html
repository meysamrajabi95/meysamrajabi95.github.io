Research

Understanding how wakes develop, how they load nearby structures, and what their signals can tell us about the surrounding flow.

01.1 / WAKE DYNAMICS

Wake dynamics & hydrodynamic loading

Wakes contain separated shear layers and unsteady vortices. Their evolution changes the pressure and viscous stresses acting on a structure, producing mean and fluctuating forces.

I study how nearby moving bodies and boundaries affect cylinder wakes and loading, including the roles of passage timing, wake recovery, and synchronization.

01.2 / VORTEX-INDUCED VIBRATION

Vortex-Induced Vibrations

Vortex-induced vibration arises from the interaction between vortex shedding and structural motion. My work examines the response of flexible cylinders and approaches to vibration control, with applications to marine risers, offshore structures, and flow-energy systems.

01.3 / FLOW SENSING

Flow Sensing

Changes in surface pressure can reveal information about nearby objects and flow conditions. I combine computational fluid dynamics, physics-based modeling, and data analysis to develop inverse models that infer the surrounding environment from flow-induced signals.
