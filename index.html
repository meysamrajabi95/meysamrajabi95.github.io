Biography

I am a PhD student in Ocean and Resources Engineering at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa, working with Professor Deniz Gedikli at FSINLD. I study how unsteady flows interact with structures, with a focus on wakes, hydrodynamic forces, and vortex-induced vibrations. My research combines computational fluid dynamics, experiments, and data analysis to understand these interactions and investigate approaches to flow sensing and vibration control.
